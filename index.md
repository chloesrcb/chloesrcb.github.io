## General

I am currently a **Temporary Lecturer and Researcher (ATER)** and a **PhD candidate in Statistics** at the University of Montpellier, France.
I am based at the **Institut Montpelliérain Alexander Grothendieck (IMAG)**, University of Montpellier, and **Inria Montpellier**, where I am a member of the [LEMON team](https://team.inria.fr/lemon/).

My research focuses on the **statistical modelling of high-resolution precipitation**, with a particular emphasis on **extreme values, dependence modeling and statistical downscaling**. 
I am supervised by [Gwladys Toulemonde](https://imag.umontpellier.fr/~toulemonde/index.html), [Thomas Opitz](https://biosp.mathnum.inrae.fr/homepage-thomas-opitz), and [Nicolas Meyer](https://sites.google.com/view/nicolasmeyer/accueil).


## Research interests

* Extreme Value Theory
* Dependence modeling
* Statistical downscaling
* Geostatistics
* Climate science and meteorology
