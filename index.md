## General

I am currently a **Temporary Lecturer and Researcher (ATER)** at the University of Montpellier, France.

I am based at the **Institut Montpelliérain Alexander Grothendieck (IMAG)**, University of Montpellier, and **Inria Montpellier**, where I am a member of the [LEMON team](https://team.inria.fr/lemon/).

I obtained my **PhD in Statistics** from the University of Montpellier in September 2026. My PhD thesis is entitled *Stochastic generator of precipitation at a high spatio-temporal resolution*.
My doctoral research focused on the **statistical modelling of high-resolution precipitation**, with particular emphasis on **extreme values, dependence modeling, geostatistics, and statistical downscaling**.
My PhD was supervised by [Gwladys Toulemonde](https://imag.umontpellier.fr/~toulemonde/index.html), [Thomas Opitz](https://biosp.mathnum.inrae.fr/homepage-thomas-opitz), and [Nicolas Meyer](https://sites.google.com/view/nicolasmeyer/accueil).

## Research interests

* Extreme Value Theory
* Dependence modeling
* Statistical downscaling
* Geostatistics
* Climate science and meteorology
